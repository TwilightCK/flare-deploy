# 🏆 Hackathon Demo Guide - Stake & Date

## 🎯 Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"Stake & Date is a revolutionary social platform that brings people together through crypto gaming. Instead of boring swipes, users match through quick prediction games with real stakes. Built on Flare Network using FTSO for decentralized price feeds and Smart Accounts for seamless UX."

## 🎬 Live Demo Script (5 minutes)

### Setup (Before Demo)
1. Have 2 browsers ready (Chrome + Incognito)
2. Both wallets funded with test FLR
3. Contracts deployed
4. App running on localhost:3000

### Demo Flow

**[0:00-0:30] Introduction**
- "Hi, I'm [name] and this is Stake & Date"
- "We're solving two problems: boring dating apps and crypto's high barrier to entry"
- Show the landing page

**[0:30-1:00] Connect Wallet (Browser 1)**
- Click "Connect Wallet"
- Show MetaMask connection
- "Notice we're on Flare Coston2 testnet"
- Show player stats (all zeros for new user)

**[1:00-1:30] Join Queue (Browser 1)**
- Click "Join Queue"
- "Users stake 0.01 FLR to enter matchmaking"
- Show transaction in MetaMask
- Show waiting screen with spinner

**[1:30-2:00] Second Player (Browser 2)**
- Switch to incognito browser
- Connect second wallet
- Join queue
- "When two players join, they're instantly matched!"

**[2:00-2:30] Game Start**
- Both browsers show game room
- "Players see each other's addresses"
- "The game: predict if FLR/USD will go UP or DOWN in 5 minutes"

**[2:30-3:00] Make Predictions**
- Browser 1: Click "UP"
- Browser 2: Click "DOWN"
- "Once both predict, the game starts"
- Show FTSO price feed integration

**[3:00-3:30] Game Active**
- Show countdown timer
- Show starting price
- "FTSO provides decentralized price data - no external oracles needed"
- "This is real blockchain data, not simulated"

**[3:30-4:00] Results (Fast-forward for demo)**
- "In a real game, we'd wait 5 minutes"
- "For the demo, let's fast-forward"
- Show game completion
- Explain winning scenarios:
  - Both correct: Split pot + bonus
  - Both wrong: Refund minus fee
  - One correct: Winner takes most

**[4:00-4:30] Claim Rewards**
- Click "Claim Rewards"
- Show transaction
- Show updated player stats
- "Winners can claim their earnings"

**[4:30-5:00] Wrap Up**
- "This is just the MVP"
- Show future features slide
- "Built with Flare's FTSO, Smart Accounts ready, FDC and FAssets planned"
- "Thank you!"

## 🎤 Q&A Preparation

**Q: How do you prevent bots?**
A: Phase 2 will use FDC to verify real social accounts. For now, the stake requirement creates a natural barrier.

**Q: What if price doesn't change?**
A: We use the actual price difference. Even 0.01% counts as movement. Ties are extremely rare.

**Q: How do you make money?**
A: 5% platform fee on each game. With volume, this scales well.

**Q: Why Flare?**
A: FTSO gives us decentralized price feeds without external oracles. Smart Accounts enable gasless UX. FDC and FAssets unlock future features.

**Q: What about gas fees?**
A: On Flare, gas is very cheap (~$0.001). Phase 2 will use Smart Accounts for gasless transactions.

**Q: How is this different from prediction markets?**
A: We're social-first. The game is just the icebreaker. The goal is connection, not just profit.

**Q: What about regulation?**
A: We're a gaming platform, not a gambling platform. Small stakes, skill-based, social focus.

**Q: Can you scale this?**
A: Absolutely. The smart contracts are efficient. We can handle thousands of concurrent games.
